Removed from required flow
--------------------------

- Supplement and antacid nutrient estimates: high burden, high missingness, poor user reliability.
- Detailed diet nutrient averages: high logging burden and only modest predictive gain.
- Ten-year weight change: recall reliability is poor; avoid as required input.
- Detailed activity, mood, and sleep-trouble modules: better suited as optional context.
- Alcohol grams and caffeine milligrams: better suited as optional trend records than required screening inputs.
